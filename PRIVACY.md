# Privacy Policy

**Heads Up** — Privacy Policy  
Last updated: April 8, 2026

## Overview

Heads Up is a macOS menu bar app that alerts you before upcoming calendar events. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

## Data Collection

Heads Up does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no server, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

## Calendar Access

Heads Up reads your calendar events using the macOS EventKit framework to display upcoming meetings and fire reminders. Calendar data is read locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. No calendar data is stored outside of what macOS provides natively.

## Third-Party Services

Heads Up does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs. When you click "Join Zoom," the app opens a URL in your default browser — no data is sent to Heads Up or any intermediary.

## Data Storage

Heads Up stores only your preferences (selected calendars, reminder timing, display settings) locally on your Mac using standard macOS user defaults. No data is stored in the cloud.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.
